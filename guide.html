New Student Guide

First-week checklist

  1. Confirm document and photo appointments with the school
  2. Set up a SIM card and internet
  3. Install ride-hailing and payment apps
  4. Join the club group chat and meet your seniors
  5. Get to know the campus and nearby shops and restaurants

Handy reminders

  • Back up important documents digitally and keep originals safe
  • Ask your teacher about any policy you are unsure of
  • Travel in groups when possible and use official ride apps
  • Ask for help in the group chat whenever you need
Emergency numbers: 112 (general), 110 (police), 118/119 (ambulance). Numbers may change; check official sources.
