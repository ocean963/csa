About Us

Our mission

To give Chinese students a platform for connection, mutual help and cultural sharing, while building friendship with Indonesian classmates and teachers.

What we do

  • Organize welcome events, socials and festivals
  • Compile practical info on documents, housing and daily life
  • Help students communicate with school staff
  • Hold China–Indonesia cultural exchange events

Our departments

  • Publicity: posters, social media and event coverage.
  • Events: plans and runs our events.
  • Outreach: liaises with the school, Indonesian students and partners.
  • Student Support: answers new students' questions and compiles living info.
